Requesting Review of a Book or Material

A ready-to-adopt procedure your school can post so families, staff, and community members have a clear, fair way to raise a concern about library or classroom material. Replace the highlighted parts with your school's details.

Before you adopt this: This is a starting template, not legal advice. Have your board or authorizer review it against your school's policies and Indiana requirements, then approve it as official procedure. ICIC members can reach out for help tailoring it.

1 Who to contact

Direct all requests to: [Name / role — e.g. Library Coordinator], [email], [phone]. Requests may also be dropped off at [front office location].

2 How the process works

  1. Submit a request. Complete the Reconsideration Request Form (page 2) and return it to the contact above.
  2. Acknowledgement. The school confirms receipt within [e.g. 5 school days] and explains next steps.
  3. Material stays available. The item remains in use during the review, unless [school/board policy] directs otherwise.
  4. Review committee. A committee — typically [e.g. an administrator, a teacher, a library staff member, and a parent representative] — reads/reviews the full material in context and considers the request.
  5. Decision. The committee gives a written decision to the requester within [e.g. 30 school days], with the reasons.
  6. Appeal. If the requester disagrees, they may appeal to [e.g. the head of school, then the board] by [how and by when].

3 Principles we follow

  • Every request is taken seriously and handled respectfully.
  • Decisions consider the material as a whole, not isolated passages.
  • We weigh educational value, age-appropriateness, and the range of families we serve.
  • The process is transparent, and the outcome is shared in writing.
