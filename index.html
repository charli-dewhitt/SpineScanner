Spine Scanner

Snap a shelf, get a catalog. Upload photos of your library book spines and this tool reads each title, looks up its details, and flags anything it isn't sure about before you export.

How to use Spine Scanner

From shelf photo to spreadsheet in five steps.

Taking good photos

Do
  • Stand straight in front of the shelf, camera level with the spines.
  • Fill the frame with one shelf or section at a time.
  • Turn on the lights — bright, even light reads best.
  • Hold steady and let the camera focus before tapping.
Skip
  • Angled or tilted shots — the ends get distorted.
  • Whole bookcases from across the room; spines get too small.
  • Glare from a window or flash washing out titles.
  • Blurry shots — a fuzzy title can't be read.

The five steps

  1. Upload one photo per shelf (add several at once if you like).
  2. Tap Scan spines and wait a few seconds per photo.
  3. Review the table that appears.
  4. Fix any flagged rows — the "Confidence notes" column tells you what to check. A flag is a heads-up, not an error.
  5. Tap Download CSV to save your catalog.

What to expect: titles and authors are read straight from the spines and come through reliably. ISBN, publisher, and year are looked up automatically — most well-known titles fill in, and rarer ones may not. Everything is editable before you export.

Uploading your CSV to Google Drive

Turn the downloaded file into a Google Sheet you can sort and share.

Where's the file? When you tapped Download CSV, it saved to your device's Downloads folder, named something like library-catalog-2026-08-06.csv.

Turn it into a Google Sheet

  1. Go to drive.google.com and sign in with your school Google account.
  2. Click New → File upload (top-left), then choose your CSV from Downloads.
  3. Double-click the uploaded file in Drive to open a preview.
  4. Click Open with Google Sheets at the top. Google converts it into a spreadsheet — each book on its own row.
  5. Done. Google saves it to your Drive. Click the title top-left to rename it.

Good to know

  • Columns are already set up: Title, Author, ISBN, Publisher, Year, your Notes, plus a "Needs review" flag and reason.
  • Sort or filter with Data → Create a filter — e.g. show only flagged rows.
  • Share with the green Share button, top-right.
  • Scanning more shelves later? Paste new rows into the bottom of one master Sheet to keep a single list.

On a phone or tablet? Install the free Google Sheets app and upload the CSV through the Drive app the same way. A computer is easier for a big catalog.
